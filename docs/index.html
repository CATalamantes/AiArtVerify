How it works

Five decisions behind the number

No black box hand-waving — this is the actual pipeline, in the order the decisions were made.

  1. 1
    Trained on a leaderboard of winners
    6,668 videos that already made YouTube Trending, across 109 countries, on a single snapshot day. There's no negative class in that data — so the question isn't "will you go viral," it's whether you'd top that pack or scrape the bottom of it.
  2. 2
    Two models, two jobs
    A Random Forest scores your hit probability and predicts views, likes and comments. A Linear Regression model runs alongside purely to explain why — its coefficients are easy to read, which is what powers the plain-English "what's driving this" panel below.
  3. 3
    Two gates decided what shipped
    Gate A measured whether channel size earns its place in the model — it added +0.10 AUC, so the form asks for it. Gate B measured whether content alone carries real signal — it scored 0.73 AUC against a 0.58 bar, so the advice panel is real, not invented.
  4. 4
    Every tip is cross-checked twice
    A recommendation only appears if a model counterfactual and the raw empirical hit-rate agree on direction, with at least 40 real videos backing it. Disagreement, or too little data, means the tip gets dropped — an empty list is a valid, honest answer.
  5. 5
    A leak, turned into a feature
    Video age at measurement time used to be a hidden shortcut the model leaned on. It's now exposed as a horizon you control — "predicted views at 24h / 48h / 7 days" — so the growth curve below is that same number, working for you instead of against you.

What this data can't tell you

  • Every training row already trended. This ranks you within winners — it can't tell you whether you'll break into Trending at all.
  • One snapshot day, publish dates spanning ~37 days. No seasonality is learnable.
  • Correlation, not causation. Suggestions describe what co-occurs with high placement, not what causes it.
  • No thumbnail, no audio, no watch-time — probably the three biggest real drivers, absent from the dataset entirely.
