    How it works

    A model built from real trending data

    We reverse-engineered what separates videos that trend from videos that don't, then scored nine weighted signals against it.

    01
    Collect
    6,668 trending videos pulled across 109 countries, with full metadata and performance stats.
    02
    Extract signals
    Title, tags, description, length, publish timing, category and channel authority at time of upload.
    03
    Weight & score
    Each signal is weighted against outcomes in the training set to produce a single 0–100 score.
    04
    Predict & advise
    The score maps to a percentile, a view-growth curve, best timing, and specific changes to make.

    About the data

    What this model can, and can't, tell you

    Hit or Flop is a directional signal, not a guarantee. Know the limits before you lean on it.

    ●Trending ≠ typical
    The training set is drawn from videos that reached each country's trending page, a biased sample skewed toward already-successful content.
    ●Correlation, not causation
    Metadata patterns are associated with trending outcomes, but that doesn't mean changing your title alone will make a video hit.
    ●No thumbnail or content signal
    The model only reads metadata submitted before upload, so it can't see your thumbnail, edit quality, or the video itself.
    ●A snapshot, not a stream
    Trends and algorithm behavior shift over time; the dataset reflects a fixed collection window, not real-time platform behavior.
